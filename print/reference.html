Imperator (Rome) — Reference · v1.0

Bot card play (both bots)

  1. Turn over a Directive (missions + compass).
  2. Offer: shuffle the hand, reveal 2. Surprise cards and #24 go to the Reserve (reveal another).
  3. Choose: ★ Live event → higher AP → Live event → first revealed.
  4. Live event? Play it (Bonus: event, then AP). Otherwise play for AP.
  5. Each AP: CONTINUE · PROTECT · SUBMIT · REGROUP → Directive missions in order → GATHER · CONTROL. First that can be done; 1 AP.

Missions

CONTINUE Leader's army at a Town (or Stronghold) with 1+ Siege Point, no Gauls outside, odds 1:2+ (Stronghold CS 2+), not yet rolled this card: roll again.

PROTECT If a Provincia province is lost or Gauls stand in Provincia: attack them at 1:1+ (3:1, largest, nearest) → else SWEEP lost provinces → else place in Provincia.

REGROUP A non-Caesar army in danger moves where it is safe: Caesar's space · most Roman BS · nearest. Danger = a Gallic army can reach it with ≥1.5× the Roman BS there (Roman Walled City: never).

SUBMIT Attack Gallic CUs at 3:1 BS (they must Submit). Largest, nearest. Siege too if a Town is there.

SIEGE Reach a Town with 2 MP left; only if: surrenders (3:1, any army; leaderless stack may take a 3rd Legion) · 2+ Siege Points and 1:1 · 1 point and Caesar at 2:1. Octodurus last. Skip locations rolled this card. Odds: all Romans there vs Garrison + all Gauls there. Order: surrender · next to Roman marker · most points · Celtica · best column · nearest.

STRIKE Attack Gallic CUs at 2:1 (Caesar 1.5:1). 3:1 · largest · nearest. Siege too if a Town is there.

RAID SWEEP Gallic IMs. Weight 4: Gallic province in Celtica, or Provincia · 3: other Gallic province · 2: no-Town province where Rome is ≤1 short / controls · else none.

INVADE Turn 3+, Caesar unmoved, 6 strongest CS 12+: remove a Britannia/Germania IM in reach (2 MP left), else march closer.

GATHER Leaderless Legions join the nearest leader in reach.

RULE Control Table 0–3. SETTLE/CONTROL whole table.

SWEEP: target + army = highest weight IM (no enemy CU, weights fixed at the start) reachable with 2 MP left → fewest MP → most MP left → stronger army → compass. It removes it, then keeps stepping to adjacent wanted IMs (cost + 2 each). Never into danger (except Caesar).

Control Table (Celtica → Belgica → Aquitania → Provincia, then compass)

  1. Presence: no Roman space in Aquitania / Belgica / Celtica → convert, else place there.
  2. Convert (Roman CU present), Gallic provinces first.
  3. Provincia: place in a lost Provincia province (Narbonensis first).
  4. Gain: place to complete control in Gaul (1 short, no Fortified Town).
  5. Deny: uncontrolled province of Gaul where Gaul is 1 short.
  6. Advance: no-Town, non-Roman province needing fewest IMs.
  7. Anywhere in Gaul next to most Roman markers; anywhere.

Between card plays

  • Replacements: reduced Legion with Caesar, then largest stack; then rebuild with Caesar. No Emergency.
  • Caesar: largest Legion stack in a friendly space with a path to Rome. Legates: largest leaderless stack, else Caesar.
  • Winter camp (full Legions first, 3 MP, forage limits): neutral Gaul space next to Roman marker → Roman IM in Roman province → Walled City → other Roman IM → neutral Gaul. Nearest, northernmost.
  • Keep the Legate with the best rating if 2+. Never plays Winter Campaign as an interrupt.

Concilium (Gaul) — Reference · v1.0

Missions (after the Directive's list: AGITATE)

RISE Turn 4+, 2+ AP, 2+ Submitted Tribes with a space: Major Uprising (2 AP; Council IMs each remove a Roman IM in Gaul, Roman provinces first). Else, a Council Tribe may come out, or 3+ Council IMs (any on its last card): empty the Box (1 AP).

AMBUSH Attack Roman CUs at 1.5:1 BS (Caesar's army 2.1:1). Caesar · largest · nearest.

RAID SWEEP Roman IMs: 4 Narbonensis · 3 Roman province · 2 other Provincia · 1 else. Never in/next to Caesar's space. Never into danger: a Roman army could reach it with ≥ the Gallic BS there (own Fortified Town: safe).

AGITATE One Agitate Table action.

BANK Turns 1–5: an IM into the Council Box (max 6).

Agitate Table (ties: compass)

  1. Convert a Roman IM where Gallic CUs are; Roman provinces first.
  2. Provincia: place there (adjacency rule), Narbonensis first.
  3. Gain: place to complete Gallic control; Celtica first.
  4. Deny: no-Town province where 1 Roman IM would give Rome control.
  5. Cut: vacant space next to 2+ Roman IMs (most first).
  6. Non-Gallic province of Gaul nearest to Gallic control; then a Gallic province; anywhere.

Tribes and leaders

  • Keep the 3 strongest Tribes with a legal space. Place each in the legal space farthest from any Legion (count to 4), then fewest adjacent Roman markers, then northernmost. Council Box only if no legal space.
  • Ambiorix / Vercingetorix: Gallic-controlled space in their Region with most Gallic CS, Fortified Town first.
  • No Spring Muster moves.

Reactions (both bots)

  • Inside own fort if enemy BS ≥ own BS.
  • Sizing up an attack: count every enemy CU in the target space, inside a fort or not.
  • Avoid Battle if enemy BS ≥ 1.5× own: to friendly-controlled, then own CUs, then compass.
  • Intercept (one stack, strongest): Rome at 2×; Gaul at 1.5× (2× vs Caesar). Units inside forts don't intercept or count.
  • Back up when intercepted if weaker.
  • Rerolls: own lower die if ≤3; else your higher die if 5–6; else pass.
  • Losses: flip full units (non-elite, weakest) → Mercenaries → reduced (weakest) → Gallic leader.
  • Retreat: into own fort here; else no enemy CUs → friendly-controlled → own CUs → not across the Channel → compass.
  • Reserve: #52 next close battle (Rome: or next siege); #54 +2 own avoid/intercept; #55 discard next play; #24 cancel list.
