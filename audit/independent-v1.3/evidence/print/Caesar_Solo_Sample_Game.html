Sample game: Imperator (level 3) vs Concilium (level 4, standard rules)

Both sides are bots, so every decision below follows the printed rules. Seed 57 (bots v1.3). Imperator gets +1 AP on every card play (its level 3); Concilium plays the standard rules (its level 4: Rome needs 12 VP). Lines starting with a Directive show the card turned over, the two cards of the Offer (* = Event Live for the bot) and the card chosen. "AP:" lines show the mission that spent each Action Point. Moves list the leader, the number of CUs, start and end space and the operations performed. Turns 1 and 2 are shown in full; later turns list only the cards played, combat, sieges, Tribes and VP.

How this game went

  • Turn 1. For the Glory of Caesar sends Caesar and all six Legions to Octodurus. On the fourth siege roll he subjugates it and opens Caesar's Pass (+1 VP). Concilium raids in Belgica, removing Roman IMs at Condrusi and Segni. Rome leads Celtica 6–1 but holds no Belgic province (0–3): Governance 8, +1 VP.
  • Turn 2. Segni surrenders to Rome. Isolation removes two cut-off Gallic IMs. Belgica is still only 1–3: Governance 8, +1 VP.
  • Turn 3. Imperator's Control Table puts Belgica, the lagging region, first. A siege brings down the Nervii's Fortified Town, and Belgica turns 4–2: Governance 10, +2 VP.
  • Turn 4. The Treveri Submit on arrival. Concilium plays Unrest in Provincia, but Imperator cancels it with #24, which an earlier Offer had revealed into its Reserve. A Gallic attack at Bellovaci is beaten off. Caesar, with odds of 2:1, besieges Eburovices for three rolls until it falls and the Aulerci are eliminated. Governance 10, +2 VP, for 7 VP.
  • Turn 5. The Arverni Submit on arrival. Caesar's twelve-strength army forces Gallic stacks to Submit at Avaricum and then at Redones, where Vercingetorix is lost. Curiosolites surrenders. But Gallic raids have cost Rome control of Volcae in Provincia, so Governance is 9. That still gives +2 VP, for 9 VP.
  • Turn 6. The Parisii, Lingones and Suessiones all Submit as they arrive, and Rome holds Celtica and Belgica to the end. Volcae stays Gallic, so Governance is 9 again: +2 VP, for 11 VP. Rome finishes one point short, and Gaul wins. The two thin winters at the start, when Belgica was Gallic, decided the game.

What this game shows about the bots:

  • Imperator. It wins provinces with RULE and RAID. Caesar's army forces Submissions and takes Fortified Towns at 2:1, and the Control Table always works first on the region Rome is losing. Its Reaction card works only once an Offer has revealed it.
  • Concilium. It raids wherever the Legions are not and never sweeps into danger, so there was only one battle in the whole game. It goes after Rome's weak points: Belgica early, then Provincia.
  • Governance decides the race, turn by turn. Rome needs dominance in both Celtica and Belgica, and Provincia intact, nearly every winter. Each winter short of that costs a VP.

How to read the log

  • A Directive line shows the Directive card turned over and its missions, then the Offer of two cards (* marks a card whose Event is Live for the bot) and the card chosen.
  • A plays line gives the card and how it was used: as an Event, as a Bonus card (Event, then AP), or for AP.
  • An AP: line names the mission that spent one Action Point (rules 2.5).
  • A move line gives the leader, the number of units moving with him, the start and end spaces, and the operations done on the way.
  • A battle line reads attacker BS vs defender BS: hits scored by the attacker / hits scored by the defender → winner (A or D).
  • A siege line gives the Siege Table column, the roll, the attacker's losses and the Siege Points before and after.
  • Turns 1 and 2 are shown in full. Later turns show only the cards played, combat, sieges, Tribe arrivals and VP.
